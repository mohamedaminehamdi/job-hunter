greenhouse / lever / ashby take the board slug out of the board's own URL - jobs.lever.co/acme. They are plain public APIs.

pages takes any URL that lists jobs, read with a browser.

locations are searched one by one. Qualify a city with its country - Zurich, Switzerland - because LinkedIn's guest search resolves a bare city name badly, and quietly answers with a different place.

linkedin / indeed are off until you set them to true. Both prohibit automated access in their terms, and both may show a wall instead of results - the risk is to your own account. indeed is blocked by its anti-bot challenge nearly every time; treat it as off.
