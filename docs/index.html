How it works

You bring the link. It brings the evidence.

STEP 1

Drop your CV in, once

It becomes one profile you check and approve. Every application after that is built from it, so you never retype your history.

STEP 2

Paste a job link

Opened in your own browser, so you get the real description and not a loading spinner. Any board, any URL.

STEP 3

Get the CV and the letter

Built for that posting out of your own work, as PDF and markdown, in a folder for that job.

STEP 4

Get who to message, and what to say

The people worth contacting, the searches that find them, and a draft specific enough to answer.

Questions

The ones worth asking first.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

Not yet — that is on the way, for the boards that allow it. Today it prepares the application and hands it to you.

Do I need to pay for anything?

No. Your coding agent is the model, so whatever you already pay for covers it. There is no provider to sign up to and no token bill.

Can I just use one skill?

Yes. Each folder carries its own copy of the library and imports nothing from its siblings, so one installed alone works exactly the same as all twelve.

Will it make my CV good?

It will make your CV accurate, and put your strongest evidence where a reader meets it. It cannot give you experience you do not have, and it will tell you plainly when a job needs some.
